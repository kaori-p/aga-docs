# Login

> **Who uses this**: All clinic staff

## Overview

The Login page is the first screen you see when you visit the AGA web app. Enter your email and password to access the system. If you forget your password, you can request a reset link.

---

## How to Log In

1. Open the AGA web app in your browser
2. Enter your **Email address**
3. Enter your **Password**
4. Click the **Log In** button

If your credentials are correct, you will be taken to the main Calendar screen.

---

## Login Form Fields

| Field | Required? | Rules |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| Email | Yes | Must be a valid email address format (e.g. name@example.com) |
| Password | Yes | Cannot be empty |

### Validation

- If you leave the **Email** field empty or enter an incorrectly formatted address, an error message appears under the field before the form is submitted
- If you leave the **Password** field empty, an error message appears under the field
- The form will not submit until both fields pass validation

---

## Forgot Password

If you cannot remember your password:

1. Click the **Forgot Password** link below the login form
2. You are taken to the Forgot Password page
3. Enter your **Email address**
4. Click the **Send Reset Link** button
5. Check your email inbox for a message from AGA containing a password reset link
6. Click the link in the email — it takes you to the Reset Password page
7. Enter a **New Password** and confirm it
8. Click **Reset Password**
9. You are redirected back to the Login page — log in with your new password

### Rules for the Reset Link

- The reset link expires after a limited time. If you click an expired link, you will see an error and need to request a new one
- Each reset link can only be used once

---

## Error Messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| "Invalid email or password" | The email and password combination does not match any account | Double-check your email address and password. Use Forgot Password if needed |
| "Please enter a valid email address" | The email field is blank or incorrectly formatted | Enter a properly formatted email address |
| "This field is required" | A required field was left empty | Fill in all required fields |
| "This reset link has expired" | The password reset link is too old | Request a new reset link via Forgot Password |

---

## Tips

- Your email address and password are case-sensitive
- Contact your clinic administrator if you do not have an account or are locked out
