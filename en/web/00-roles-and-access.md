# Roles & Access

> **Who uses this**: All clinic staff

## Overview

Every staff account in the AGA web app is assigned a **role**. Your role determines which pages you can visit and which actions you can perform. Some buttons and menu items are hidden if your role does not have permission for them.

---

## Staff Roles

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Admin** | Full access to everything, including system-wide settings |
| **ClinicManager** | Manages clinic operations, staff, and settings for their clinic |
| **Chief** | Senior clinical staff with broad access to patient and scheduling features |
| **Doctor** | Medical professionals with access to patient medical records and consultations |
| **Counselor** | Hair treatment counselors with access to patient records and appointments |
| **MedicalTeam** | Nursing and clinical support staff |
| **Concierge** | Reception staff managing daily scheduling and patient coordination |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to view schedules and patient information |

## What Happens When You Try to Access a Restricted Page

If you navigate to a page your role does not have permission for, you will be redirected to a page you are allowed to access. Restricted buttons and menu items are not shown to users who cannot use them.

---

## Changing Roles

Only an **Admin** can change a staff member's role. If you need a different level of access, contact your clinic administrator.
