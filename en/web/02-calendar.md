# Calendar & Scheduling

> **Who uses this**: Concierge, Chief, Counselor, Doctor, MedicalTeam, ClinicManager, Admin
> **Connected to Mobile**: When a staff member's time is blocked or marked unavailable, patients cannot book during that time. When an appointment is marked "Started," the patient's Join Video Call button activates.

## Overview

The Calendar is the main daily operations screen. It shows every staff member's schedule as a timeline for a selected date. Staff can view, create, and manage appointments and time blocks from this screen. A multi-clinic view is also available.

---

## How to Access

After logging in, you are taken to the Calendar automatically. You can also click **Calendar** in the navigation bar.

---

## Switching Between View Modes

The calendar has several modes, which change what you can do by clicking on the timeline:

| Mode | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Preview** | View-only mode. Click anywhere to see details but not make changes |
| **Time Divider — Manual** | Click on a time slot to add a divider that splits an employee's workday into sections |
| **Time Divider — Automatic** | Set time dividers across multiple employees for multiple days at once |
| **Time Block** | Click on a time slot to block off time for an employee (marks them as unavailable) |
| **Unavailable for Mobile** | Mark specific time ranges where an employee cannot be booked by patients through the mobile app |

To change modes, use the mode selector buttons at the top of the calendar.

---

## Navigating Dates

- Use the **left arrow** and **right arrow** buttons to move to the previous or next day
- Use the **date picker** (calendar icon) to jump directly to a specific date
- The current date is highlighted

---

## What You See on the Calendar

Each row represents one **employee**. Their timeline shows:
- **Appointment blocks**: Colored blocks showing scheduled patient appointments
- **Time blocks**: Gray blocks marking unavailable time
- **Time dividers**: Lines splitting the workday into sections
- **Lunch break**: A draggable block showing the employee's lunch period

---

## Actions on the Calendar

### Adding a Time Block

A time block marks an employee as unavailable for that period.

1. Switch to **Time Block** mode
2. Click on the time slot in the employee's row where you want to add the block
3. A dialog opens. Enter:
   - **Start time** (required)
   - **End time** (required)
   - **Description** (optional)
4. Click **Save**

The block appears on the timeline immediately.

### Editing a Time Block

1. Click on an existing time block
2. A dialog opens showing the current start time, end time, and description
3. Edit the fields as needed
4. Click **Save**

### Deleting a Time Block

1. Click on the time block you want to remove
2. In the dialog, click **Delete**
3. Confirm the deletion when prompted

### Managing Time Dividers

Time dividers break an employee's workday into visible sections. They do not block appointments but help organize the timeline visually.

**Manual mode** (one day, one employee at a time):
1. Switch to **Time Divider — Manual** mode
2. Click on a time slot in an employee's row
3. A divider is added at that time

**Automatic mode** (multiple employees, multiple days at once):
1. Switch to **Time Divider — Automatic** mode
2. A dialog opens
3. Select the employees and the date range
4. Set the divider times
5. Click **Apply**

To remove a divider, click on it and select **Delete**.

### Managing Lunch Breaks

Each employee's lunch break appears as a labeled block on their timeline.

- **Move** the lunch break by dragging it to a different time slot
- **Edit** the lunch break by clicking on it and adjusting the start and end time in the dialog

### Extending or Shortening Working Hours

To adjust when an employee's workday starts or ends:

1. Click on the employee's name or the edge of their working hours block
2. A dialog appears with **Start Time** and **End Time** fields
3. Adjust the times and click **Save**

### Ending an Employee's Work Shift

To mark that an employee has finished their shift for the day:

1. Click on the employee's row (in Preview mode)
2. Find the **End Shift** option in the menu
3. Confirm the action

## Appointment Actions

### Viewing Appointment Details

Click on any appointment block in Preview mode to see:
- Patient name and contact info
- Appointment type
- Assigned room
- Start and end time

### Quick Editing an Appointment

From the appointment detail popup:
1. Click **Edit**
2. A quick-edit dialog opens
3. Change the appointment type, time, or assigned staff member
4. Click **Save**

### Moving an Appointment to a Different Staff Member

1. Click on the appointment block
2. Click **Move**
3. Select the target staff member from the list
4. Confirm

### Swapping Two Appointments Between Employees

1. Click on the first appointment
2. Click **Swap**
3. Click on the second appointment (belonging to a different employee)
4. Confirm the swap

Both appointments exchange their time slots and assigned staff members.

### Canceling an Appointment

1. Click on the appointment block
2. Click **Cancel Appointment**
3. A confirmation dialog appears — review the appointment details
4. Click **Confirm Cancel**

> **Important**: Canceling an appointment cannot be undone. The patient will be notified.

---

## Marking a Staff Member as Unavailable for Mobile

When a staff member is marked unavailable for mobile during a time period, patients cannot book them through the mobile app during that time.

1. Switch to **Unavailable for Mobile** mode
2. Click on the time range in the employee's row
3. The system marks that period as unavailable for mobile booking

---

## All Salons View

The **All Salons** view shows employee timelines across all clinic locations on one screen.

- Access it via the **All Salons** tab or button in the navigation
- The same calendar interactions (time blocks, time dividers, appointment edits) are available in this view
- Employees are grouped by clinic location

---

## Real-Time Updates

The calendar updates automatically without needing to refresh the page. If another staff member makes changes (such as editing an appointment or adding a time block), you will see those changes appear on your screen within a few seconds.

---

## Unsaved Changes Warning

If you have made changes that have not been saved and attempt to leave the calendar or switch dates, a confirmation dialog will appear:

- **Leave without saving**: Your changes will be lost
- **Stay and save**: You remain on the page to complete your changes

Always save your work before navigating away.

---

## Tips & Notes

- The calendar works best on a wide screen — scroll horizontally to see all staff members on busy days
- If an appointment block appears grayed out, it may already be completed or canceled
- Time blocks and dividers are visible to all staff who view the calendar
- Appointments created or edited here are reflected immediately in the patient's mobile app
