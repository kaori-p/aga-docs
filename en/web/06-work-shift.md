# Work Shift

> **Who uses this**: Concierge, ClinicManager, Admin

## Overview

The Work Shift screen shows a weekly grid of staff assignments across all clinic locations. Use it to assign working shifts, manage which employees work on which days, and keep schedules accurate for the week.

---

## How to Access

Click **Work Shift** in the top navigation bar.

---

## What You See

The screen shows a **weekly grid**:
- Rows represent each **employee**
- Columns represent each **day of the week** (Monday through Sunday)
- Each cell shows whether the employee is assigned to work that day and at which clinic

Clinic locations are shown in separate **tabs** at the top. Each tab represents one clinic.

---

## Navigating Weeks

- Click the **left arrow** to go to the previous week
- Click the **right arrow** to go to the next week
- Use the **month/year selector** to jump to a specific month and then navigate by week

---

## Non-Working Days

Days marked as non-working (clinic holidays) are displayed in a **grayed-out** style. You cannot assign shifts on non-working days.

---

## Assigning a Shift

To assign a working shift to an employee for a specific day:

1. Click on the cell at the intersection of the employee's row and the target day
2. A dialog opens
3. Select the **shift type** or **time period** for the assignment
4. Click **Save**

The cell updates to show the assignment.

---

## Removing or Changing a Shift

1. Click on a cell that already has an assignment
2. The dialog opens showing the current shift
3. Change the shift details, or click **Remove** to clear the assignment
4. Click **Save**

---

## Saving Changes

Changes you make are **not saved automatically**. You must click the **Save All Changes** button at the top of the screen to apply your edits.

To discard all unsaved changes, click **Discard**.

### Unsaved Changes Warning

If you try to navigate to a different week while you have unsaved changes, a confirmation dialog appears:

- **Leave and discard changes**: Your edits will be lost
- **Stay on this week**: You remain on the current week so you can save

---

## Error Indicators

If a shift assignment has a conflict or problem (e.g., an employee is assigned to two clinics at the same time), a **red indicator** appears on the clinic tab. Click the tab to see which assignments need to be corrected.

---

## Tips & Notes

- Always click **Save All Changes** before switching to a different week — unsaved changes are not preserved
- The work shift schedule affects what staff appear on the Calendar for a given day
- Non-working days are configured in [Staff Management](10-staff-management.md)
