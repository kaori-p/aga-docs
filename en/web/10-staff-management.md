# Staff Management

> **Who uses this**: ClinicManager, Admin

## Overview

The Staff Management section lets clinic managers create and manage staff accounts, configure the medicine inventory, and set non-working days for the clinic. It is accessed through the **Manage** menu.

---

## How to Access

Click **Manage** in the top navigation bar.

---

## Managing Staff by Role

The Manage section is divided into tabs for each staff role:

- **Clinic Managers**
- **Concierges**
- **Counselors**
- **Doctors**
- **Medical Team**
- **Viewers**

Each tab shows a list of staff members with that role. The actions available are the same across all tabs.

### Adding a New Staff Member

1. Click the **Add** button on the relevant role tab
2. Fill in the staff member's details:
   - Full name (required)
   - Email address (required) — this will be their login email
   - Phone number (optional)
   - Any role-specific information (e.g., specialty for doctors)
3. Click **Save**

The new staff member receives an email invitation to set their password and log in.

### Editing a Staff Member

1. Find the staff member in the list
2. Click the **Edit** icon in their row
3. Update any details
4. Click **Save**

### Deleting a Staff Member

1. Find the staff member in the list
2. Click the **Delete** icon in their row
3. Confirm the deletion

> **Note**: Deleting a staff member removes their login access. Historical records they were involved in are not deleted.

### Assigning Availability and Schedules

For certain roles (especially doctors and counselors), you can configure their available days and working hours. These appear on the Calendar for appointment booking.

---

## Medicine Inventory

The **Medicines** tab lets you manage the list of medicines available for prescriptions and orders.

### Adding a New Medicine

1. Click the **Medicines** tab
2. Click **Add Medicine**
3. Fill in:
   - Medicine name (required)
   - Description (optional)
   - Unit price (required) — how much the medicine costs per unit
   - Stock quantity (required) — how many units are currently available
4. Click **Save**

### Editing a Medicine

1. Find the medicine in the list
2. Click **Edit**
3. Update the price, stock, or other details
4. Click **Save**

### Deleting a Medicine

1. Find the medicine in the list
2. Click **Delete** and confirm

> **Note**: If a medicine is included in an active subscription, it cannot be deleted until removed from the subscription.

---

## Non-Working Days

Set clinic-wide holidays and closure days. Non-working days appear grayed out on the Calendar and Work Shift screens, and patients cannot book appointments on those days.

### Adding a Non-Working Day

1. Click the **Non-Working Days** tab
2. Click **Add Non-Working Day**
3. Select the **date** from the calendar picker
4. Optionally add a **reason** or label (e.g., "National Holiday")
5. Click **Save**

### Removing a Non-Working Day

1. Find the day in the list
2. Click **Delete** and confirm

The day becomes a regular working day again.

---

## Tips & Notes

- Only **ClinicManager** and **Admin** roles can access the Manage section
- Changes to staff records take effect immediately — new staff can log in as soon as they set their password
- Medicine prices set here are used when calculating subscription totals and order costs
- Non-working days affect all clinics or specific clinics depending on your configuration
