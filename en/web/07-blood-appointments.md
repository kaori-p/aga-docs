# Blood Appointments

> **Who uses this**: Concierge, Counselor, Doctor, MedicalTeam, Chief, ClinicManager, Admin

## Overview

The Blood Appointments screen shows a daily calendar of blood test appointments. Use it to see which patients have blood tests scheduled on a given day and to track past and upcoming blood examinations.

---

## How to Access

Click **Blood Appointments** in the top navigation bar.

---

## What You See

The screen displays:
- A **date header** showing the currently selected date
- A **list of blood test appointments** for that date, showing:
  - Patient name
  - Appointment time
  - Whether the appointment is in the past or upcoming

---

## Navigating Dates

| Control | Action |
|---------|--------|
| **Left arrow** | Go to the previous day |
| **Right arrow** | Go to the next day |
| **Go to Today** button | Jump back to today's date |
| **Date picker** | Click the date shown to open a calendar and jump to any date |

---

## Appointment Status

- **Upcoming appointments** are shown normally
- **Past appointments** are shown in a subdued style to indicate they have already occurred

---

## Viewing More Appointments

If there are many appointments for a selected date, click **Load More** at the bottom of the list to see additional entries.

---

## Tips & Notes

- Blood appointment records are read-only on this screen — to enter blood test results for a specific patient, go to the patient's medical record via [Patient Management](03-patient-management.md) → Blood Examination
- This screen is useful for at-a-glance daily planning and confirming which patients need blood tests on a given day
