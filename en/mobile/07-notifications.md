# Notifications

> **Who uses this**: Patients
> **Connected to Web**: Notifications are sent by the clinic from the Admin panel on the web app, or are triggered automatically by system events (such as order status changes).

## Overview

The Notifications tab shows messages and updates sent by the clinic. You may also receive push notifications on your phone even when the app is closed.

---

## How to Access

Tap the **Notifications** tab (second tab) in the bottom navigation bar.

---

## Notification Badge

A **badge** (a small number) appears on the Notifications tab when you have unread notifications. The number shows how many unread notifications you have. When you open the Notifications tab, all currently visible notifications are automatically marked as read and the badge clears.

---

## What You See

The Notifications screen shows a scrollable list of notifications:

- Each notification shows a **title**, a **preview of the message**, and the **date** it was received
- Notifications are grouped by date (e.g., "Today", "Yesterday", specific dates)

---

## Reading a Notification

Tap any notification in the list to open it and read the full message. The notification is marked as read when you tap it.

---

## Loading More Notifications

The list shows the most recent notifications first. To see older ones:
- Scroll to the bottom of the list
- Additional notifications load automatically as you scroll

---

## Push Notifications

Even when the app is closed or running in the background, you may receive push notifications from the clinic. These appear on your phone's lock screen or notification center like any other message.

- Tap a push notification to open the app and go directly to the relevant screen
- Push notifications are also visible in the Notifications tab inside the app

---

## Types of Notifications You May Receive

- **Appointment reminders**: A reminder before your upcoming appointment
- **Order status updates**: When your medicine order is shipped or has a payment due
- **Clinic messages**: General announcements or personalized messages from the clinic
- **Content cards**: Special offers or informational content from the clinic

---

## Tips & Notes

- You need to allow the app to send notifications when prompted. If you accidentally denied notification permissions, go to your phone's Settings to enable them again
- If you are not receiving push notifications, check that notifications are enabled for the AGA app in your phone's Settings
- Older notifications remain visible in the list until they are archived by the clinic
