# Medicine Orders

> **Who uses this**: Patients
> **Connected to Web**: Medicines are prescribed by the doctor after your consultation on the web app. Subscription orders are automatically created from recurring subscriptions set up by staff. When staff ship your order, the status updates to "Shipped" here.

## Overview

The Orders tab shows your medicine delivery orders. After a consultation, your doctor prescribes medicines that you can order for home delivery. You can also track the status of your current and past orders.

---

## How to Access

Tap the **Orders** tab (third tab) in the bottom navigation bar.

The Orders tab shows a badge number when you have a pending payment that needs attention.

---

## Order States

Your latest order is shown on this screen. The display changes based on the order's current state:

| State | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| **No Order** | You do not have any medicine orders yet. An option to create one may appear. |
| **Pending Payment** | Your order is ready but has not been paid yet. Tap to pay. |
| **Paid** | Payment was received. Your order will be shipped soon. |
| **Shipped** | Your medicines are on their way. |
| **Expired** | The order period has passed without payment. This order is no longer valid. |
| **Canceled** | The order was canceled. |

---

## Creating a Medicine Order

After your consultation, medicines prescribed by your doctor become available to order.

1. Tap **Create Order** (visible when you have no active order)
2. A list of available medicines appears, grouped by type:
   - **Prescribed medicines**: Medicines your doctor has prescribed, showing how many units remain
   - **Non-prescribed supplements**: Other products available for purchase
3. Adjust quantities using the **+** and **−** buttons next to each medicine
4. The price for each item and the **total** are shown and update as you adjust quantities
5. Tap **Confirm Order** to place the order

The order is now in **Pending Payment** state — see [Payment](06-payment.md) to complete your purchase.

---

## Subscription Orders

If the clinic has set up a **recurring subscription** for you, medicine orders will appear automatically in the Orders tab on a regular schedule (set by the clinic). You do not need to create these orders manually — they appear and wait for your payment.

See [Payment](06-payment.md) to pay for a subscription order.

---

## Viewing Order Details

The Orders screen shows:
- The list of medicines in your current order
- Quantity and cost per item
- Subtotal, shipping cost, and total
- Order creation date and clinic location

---

## Tips & Notes

- You can only have one active order at a time — complete payment on your current order before a new one can be created
- Expired orders cannot be paid — contact the clinic if your order expired before you could pay
- If you do not see any medicines in the order screen, your doctor may not have written a prescription yet — check with your clinic after your consultation
- Orders from subscriptions and one-time orders both appear in the same Orders tab
