# Getting Started

> **Who uses this**: Patients (new users)

## Overview

This guide helps you download the AGA mobile app and get ready to use it for the first time.

---

## Downloading the App

### iPhone (iOS)
1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone
2. Search for **AGA**
3. Tap **Get** to download and install the app
4. Once installed, tap the AGA icon on your home screen to open it

### Android
1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android phone
2. Search for **AGA**
3. Tap **Install**
4. Once installed, tap the AGA icon to open it

---

## First Launch

When you open the app for the first time, you will see a loading screen while the app starts up. After a few seconds, you will be taken to the **Login screen**.

If this is your first time using AGA, you will need an account. Contact your clinic — they will register you as a patient first before you can log in.

---

## Special Screens

### Force Update

If your version of the app is too old, you will see a **Force Update** screen instead of the normal app. This means the app needs to be updated before you can continue.

**What to do:**
1. Tap the **Update** button on the screen
2. You will be taken to the App Store or Google Play
3. Install the update
4. Reopen the app

### Maintenance

If the app is undergoing maintenance, you will see a **Maintenance** screen. You cannot use the app during this time.

**What to do:**
- Wait until maintenance is complete
- Check any messages from the clinic for an estimated end time
- Restart the app after some time to check if maintenance has finished
