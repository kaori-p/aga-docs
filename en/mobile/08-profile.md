# Profile

> **Who uses this**: Patients

## Overview

The Profile tab shows your account information and gives you access to account-level actions such as editing your email, contacting the clinic, or logging out.

---

## How to Access

Tap the **Profile** tab (fourth tab, rightmost) in the bottom navigation bar.

---

## What You See

| Information | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Full Name | Your registered name (cannot be edited in the app; contact the clinic to update) |
| Phone Number | Your registered phone number (cannot be edited; contact the clinic to change) |
| Email Address | Your email address (can be edited) |

---

## Editing Your Email Address

1. Tap **Edit** (or the edit icon next to the email field)
2. The email field becomes editable
3. Clear the current email and type your new address
4. Tap **Save**

### Email Validation Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Format | Must be a valid email format (e.g., name@example.com) |
| Uniqueness | The email must not already be registered to another account |
| Optional | You may leave the email field empty if you do not have one |

### Error Messages for Email

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| "Please enter a valid email address" | The format is incorrect | Check for typos and ensure the address includes @ and a domain |
| "This email is already in use" | Another account has this email registered | Use a different email address or contact the clinic |

---

## Calling the Clinic

Tap the **Call Clinic** button to open your phone's dialer with the clinic's phone number pre-filled. Tap the call button in the dialer to connect.

---

## Accessing Medical Records / Media

From the Profile tab, you can access a link to your medical records or media files. Tap the relevant button to view your clinical documents and images.

---

## Logging Out

1. Tap the **Logout** button (usually in the top-right corner of the Profile screen)
2. A confirmation prompt may appear — confirm the logout
3. You are returned to the Login screen

> **Note**: After logging out, you will need to log in again using your phone number and a new verification code.

---

## Tips & Notes

- Your name and phone number are set by the clinic when your patient record was created. To update them, contact the clinic directly
- Keeping your email up to date helps the clinic reach you with important communications
- Logging out does not delete your account or any of your medical records
