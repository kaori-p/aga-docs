# Video Call

> **Who uses this**: Patients
> **Connected to Web**: Clinic staff start the appointment from the web Calendar. Once they mark it as "Started," your Join Video Call button becomes active.

## Overview

Online consultations at AGA are conducted via video call inside the app. You can join a video call when your appointment is in progress and you have granted the app permission to use your camera and microphone.

---

## Before You Can Make a Video Call

The app needs permission to use your device's **camera** and **microphone**. You will be asked to grant these permissions the first time you try to join a call.

### Required Permissions

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|------------|-----------------|
| Camera | So the doctor can see you during the consultation |
| Microphone | So you and the doctor can hear each other |
| Phone State (Android only) | To manage the call when the phone rings |
| Bluetooth (Android 12 and later) | To support audio output through Bluetooth headphones |

### How to Grant Permissions

1. Tap **Join Video Call** from the Appointments screen
2. A **Permissions** screen appears listing the required permissions
3. Tap **Enable Permission**
4. Your phone's system dialog appears — tap **Allow**
5. Repeat for each permission requested

Once all permissions are granted, you proceed to the video call.

> **Note**: If you tap **Deny** on a permission, you will not be able to join the call. You will need to go into your phone's Settings and enable the permissions manually.

### Enabling Permissions Manually (if denied)

**iPhone:**
1. Go to **Settings** on your phone
2. Scroll down and tap **AGA**
3. Turn on **Camera** and **Microphone**

**Android:**
1. Go to **Settings** → **Apps** (or Application Manager)
2. Find **AGA** and tap it
3. Tap **Permissions**
4. Enable **Camera** and **Microphone**

---

## How to Join a Video Call

1. Open the **Appointments** tab
2. Wait until your appointment status shows **"Started"** — this means the clinic staff has begun the session
3. Tap **Join Video Call**
4. Grant any required permissions (first time only)
5. The video call begins

---

## During the Call

The video call screen shows:
- **Your video**: A small preview of yourself in one corner
- **Doctor's video**: The main view showing the other participant
- **Call controls**: Buttons to mute/unmute your microphone, turn your camera on/off, and end the call

---

## Ending the Call

Tap the **End Call** button (usually a red phone icon) to leave the call. The consultation is now complete.

---

## Tips & Notes

- Make sure you have a stable internet connection before joining a call — Wi-Fi is recommended
- Find a quiet, well-lit place for your call
- The **Join Video Call** button only appears when your appointment status is "Started" — if you do not see it, your appointment has not started yet
- If the call quality is poor, try moving closer to your Wi-Fi router or switching to a stronger connection
- If the call drops, reopen the app and tap **Join Video Call** again to reconnect
