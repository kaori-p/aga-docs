# Payment

> **Who uses this**: Patients
> **Connected to Web**: When you complete payment, staff can see the updated payment status in the patient management screen.

## Overview

After creating a medicine order, you need to pay for it before it is shipped. The app supports three payment methods: bank transfer, credit/debit card, or free delivery (when applicable).

---

## How to Access

When your order is in **Pending Payment** state:
1. Tap the **Orders** tab
2. Tap **Pay Now** (or the order details area)
3. The payment screen opens

---

## Payment Methods

### Bank Transfer

1. Tap **Bank Transfer**
2. The bank account details are shown on screen:
   - Bank name
   - Branch name
   - Account type
   - Account number
   - Account holder name
3. Use your bank's app, ATM, or online banking to transfer the exact amount shown to this account
4. Return to the AGA app and tap **I Have Transferred** (or a similar confirmation button)

> **Important**: Your order will not be shipped until the clinic confirms receipt of the transfer. This may take 1–3 business days. Use the exact amount shown — do not round up or down.

### Card Payment

1. Tap **Card Payment**
2. You can choose a saved card or add a new one

**Using a Saved Card:**
- Your saved cards are listed with the card brand (Visa, Mastercard, etc.), last 4 digits, and expiry date
- Tap the card you want to use
- Tap **Pay**

**Adding a New Card:**
1. Tap **Add New Card**
2. Fill in the card details:

| Field | Required? | Rules |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| Cardholder Name | Yes | Full name as it appears on the card |
| Card Number | Yes | 16-digit card number (formatted automatically as you type) |
| Expiry Date | Yes | MM/YY format (e.g., 03/27) |
| CVC / Security Code | Yes | 3-digit code on the back of the card (4 digits for American Express) |
| Save this card | Optional | Check this box to save the card for future payments |

3. Tap **Pay** to complete the payment

### Free Delivery

When your order qualifies for free delivery:
1. Tap **Free Delivery**
2. A confirmation button appears
3. Tap **Confirm** to process the free order

---

## Payment Success Screen

After a successful payment, a confirmation screen appears:
- A message confirming your payment was received
- A button to **Create a New Appointment**
- A button to **View Payment History**

Tap either button, or tap the back arrow to return to the Orders tab.

---

## Saved Cards

You can manage your saved cards from the payment screen. Saved cards display:
- Card brand logo (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, etc.)
- Last 4 digits of the card number
- Expiry date

---

## Error Messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| "Card declined" | The card payment was rejected by the card issuer | Check your card details, ensure the card has sufficient funds, or use a different card |
| "Card has expired" | The expiry date on the card has passed | Use a different, valid card |
| "Invalid card number" | The card number entered is not a valid format | Double-check the card number and try again |
| "Invalid CVC" | The security code is incorrect | Check the CVC on the back of the card and re-enter it |
| "Payment failed" | A general payment error occurred | Try again, or try a different payment method. Contact the clinic if the problem continues |

---

## Tips & Notes

- Card payments are processed immediately — your order status updates right away
- Bank transfers may take 1–3 business days to be confirmed — your order remains in "Pending" state until confirmed by the clinic
- Saving your card makes future payments faster — your full card number is never stored in the app; only a secure token is kept
- If you need a receipt or payment confirmation for tax purposes, contact the clinic
