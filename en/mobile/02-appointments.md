# Appointments

> **Who uses this**: Patients
> **Connected to Web**: Appointment slots are created by clinic staff on the web Calendar. When staff mark an appointment as "Started," your Join Video Call button appears.

## Overview

The Appointments tab is the home screen of the app. It shows your current appointment status and lets you book new consultations with the clinic.

---

## How to Access

Tap the **Appointments** tab (first tab) in the bottom navigation bar.

---

## Bottom Navigation Tabs

The app has four main sections accessible from the bottom of the screen:

| Tab | What it contains |
|-----|-----------------|
| **Appointments** | Your consultation status and booking |
| **Notifications** | Messages from the clinic |
| **Orders** | Medicine orders and delivery status |
| **Profile** | Your account information |

The Notifications and Orders tabs show a **badge** (a small number) when there are unread notifications or pending payments.

---

## Appointment States

The Appointments screen looks different depending on your current situation:

### No Appointment
You do not have any upcoming appointments yet.
- A carousel of content cards from the clinic is displayed
- A button to **create your first appointment** appears
- A link to a tutorial ("First Online" walkthrough) may appear

### Upcoming Appointment
You have an appointment scheduled in the future.
- The appointment details are shown: date, time, clinic location, and doctor name
- A **Join Video Call** button is available — see [Video Call](04-video-call.md)
- A **View Reception Card** button shows the clinic's physical location details

### Appointment Started
Your appointment is currently in progress (the clinic staff have marked it as started).
- A **Join Video Call** button appears — tap it to begin your online consultation

### Next Appointment
Similar to Upcoming, showing the next scheduled appointment.
- Clinic information, date, and time are displayed
- You can view the reception card or the clinic website

### Appointment Canceled
Your most recent appointment was canceled.
- The screen shows the canceled appointment details
- A button to **book a new appointment** appears

### First Appointment Canceled
Your very first appointment was canceled.
- A special message and a button to create a new booking appear

## Booking an Appointment

Tap the **Book Appointment** or **Create Appointment** button. A multi-step booking flow opens:

**Step 1 — Select Clinic**
- Choose from available clinic locations
- Regular clinics and salon locations may be listed separately

**Step 2 — Select Appointment Type**
- Choose the type of consultation you need (e.g., online, in-person)
- Available types depend on the selected clinic

**Step 3 — Select Date and Time**
- A calendar shows available dates
- Tap a date to see available time slots for that day
- Tap a time slot to select it
- Only slots marked as available by clinic staff can be booked

**Step 4 — Confirm**
- Review your selection: clinic, type, date, time
- Tap **Confirm** to complete the booking

Your appointment is now scheduled. It appears on the Appointments screen and the clinic's web Calendar.

### Booking Rules

- You cannot book a time slot in the past
- You cannot book a slot that has already been taken
- Some time slots may be unavailable because staff have marked them as blocked or unavailable for mobile booking
- If no slots are shown for a date, there is no available time for that day — try a different date

---

## Re-booking After Cancellation

If your appointment was canceled, you can book a new one:

1. Tap **Book New Appointment** on the screen
2. Follow the same booking steps above

> **Note**: There may be restrictions on re-booking depending on clinic policy. If the booking button is not available, contact the clinic directly.

---

## Viewing Reception Card

The reception card shows the clinic's address, floor, and any directions for visiting in person.

- Tap **View Reception Card** from the appointment details
- The card is displayed on screen — take a screenshot or note the details before your visit

---

## Creating a Medicine Order

From the Appointments screen, there is also a button to **Create Medicine Order** (visible after a consultation). This takes you to the Orders tab to select and order your prescribed medicines. See [Medicine Orders](05-medicine-orders.md) for details.

---

## Tips & Notes

- Keep the app open or check it before your appointment — the **Join Video Call** button only appears when the clinic staff starts the session
- If you cannot see an available time slot, contact the clinic — slots may be added by staff
- The appointment tab badge number shows if there is an action needed
