# Interview Sheet

> **Who uses this**: Patients
> **Connected to Web**: After you submit an interview sheet, clinic staff can view your answers in your medical record on the web app.

## Overview

The interview sheet is a health questionnaire that the clinic asks you to complete. It collects information about your health history, symptoms, and concerns so that the clinic can prepare for your consultation.

---

## How to Access

The interview sheet is accessible from the Appointments screen or may appear automatically when you are prompted to complete it before a consultation.

---

## How to Complete the Interview Sheet

The sheet is divided into multiple pages. Answer all questions on each page before moving to the next.

### Page Navigation

- The current page number and total number of pages are shown at the top (e.g., "Page 1 of 3")
- Tap **Next** to move to the next page (shown on all pages except the last)
- Tap **OK** on the final page to submit the sheet

You can tap the **back arrow** to return to a previous page to review or change your answers.

---

## Question Types

The interview sheet uses different types of questions:

| Question Type | How to Answer |
|---------------|---------------|
| **Text (single line)** | Type your answer using the keyboard |
| **Text (multi-line)** | Type a longer answer; the text box expands as you type |
| **Dropdown** | Tap the field and select one option from the list that appears |
| **Multiple choice** | Tap one or more options from the displayed choices |
| **Single select buttons** | Tap one button from a set of options (only one can be selected at a time) |
| **Date picker** | Tap the field and scroll to select a date |

---

## Required Fields

Some questions are **required** — you must answer them before you can proceed to the next page.

- Required questions are marked (usually with an asterisk * or a label)
- If you tap **Next** without answering a required question, the question is highlighted in red and an error message appears
- Fill in all required questions and tap **Next** again

---

## Terms of Service

On the final page, a link to the **Terms of Service** is shown. Tap the link to read the terms if you have not already.

---

## Submitting the Sheet

On the last page:
1. Ensure all required questions are answered
2. Tap **OK**
3. The sheet is submitted to the clinic

After submission:
- Your answers are sent to the clinic staff and appear in your medical record
- You cannot edit your answers after submitting

---

## Error Messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| "This field is required" | You have left a required question blank | Fill in the question and try again |
| "Please enter a valid value" | Your answer does not meet the format requirements for that question | Re-read the question and correct your answer |
| Submission error | A problem occurred while sending | Check your internet connection and try submitting again |

---

## Tips & Notes

- Take your time filling in the sheet — the clinic uses your answers to prepare for your consultation
- If you are unsure about any medical question, it is fine to leave optional questions blank
- The sheet is only submitted when you tap **OK** on the final page — navigating back to previous pages does not send your answers
